Rin privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Rin is a private assistant run by one person for personal use. It is not offered to the public.

What Rin accesses

Rin signs in only to its own Google account. Through Google's APIs it can read and change that account's Google Docs, Sheets, Drive files, Gmail and Calendar, and Apps Script projects, only to carry out requests from the people it works for.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to perform the requested action and reply to it. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone other than the person who asked and the AI model provider that processes the request. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage

The Google sign-in token is stored on the owner's private server and is never published. Content from Google is not kept beyond what the request needs.

Contact and removal

To ask about or remove data, contact the owner directly, or revoke Rin's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
